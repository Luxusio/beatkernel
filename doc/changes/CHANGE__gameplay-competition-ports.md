generic sessions and member state. The compatibility bridge specializes legacy
native names and delegates concrete IO owners to those ports. Existing public
empty-roster call inference is retained through compatibility wrappers. No
observer boxing, new crate or additional per-note virtual dispatch was added.

order and then the group observer, after the existing shared output barrier.

their delegated completion notification has no network effect. Cancellation,
closure, time cutoff and observer failures cannot mark completion.

an explicit host, virtual waits and actual portable Runtime/Mixer/capture paths.

full or partial Stop admission and original committed prefixes, plus actual
